Privacy Policy — Alarm Clock Pro

Effective date: November 2, 2026

Alarm Clock Pro ("the App") is an alarm clock with a world clock, countdown timer and stopwatch. This policy explains what information the App handles and how.

1. Information stored on your device

The App stores your alarms, repeat settings, world clock cities, preferences and any custom alarm sound files you choose only on your device (local storage). This data is never sent to us or to any server operated by us. The App works fully with no account at all; an optional sign-in is described in the next section.

2. Optional sign-in

Sign-in is optional. If you choose "Continue with Google", sign-in is handled by Supabase (our authentication provider) using Google Sign-In. Google then shares your name, email address and profile photo, and Supabase stores your account name and email so you can sign in again. We use this information only to show your signed-in profile inside the App; we do not sell it or share it for advertising. If you use "Continue as guest", the name you enter is stored only on your device. You can sign out at any time inside the App. To delete your sign-in account together with its stored name and email, contact us at the email below and we will delete it.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App shows ads provided by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use your device's advertising ID and ad-related data (such as ad impressions and interactions) to provide and measure ads, as described in Google's Privacy Policy. You can reset or limit ad personalization in your device settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads).

4. Alarms and notifications

Alarms ring on your device at the times you set. No alarm content or schedule leaves your device.

5. Data deletion

Because almost all App data is stored locally, you can delete it at any time by clearing the App's storage in your device settings or by uninstalling the App. If you created a sign-in account, contact us to delete the account data stored by Supabase, as described in Section 2.

6. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The current version will always be available at this page, with the effective date shown above.
