Privacy Policy — HabitFlow

Effective date: November 1, 2026

HabitFlow ("the App") is a habit tracker and focus timer. This policy explains what information the App handles and how.

1. Information stored on your device

The App stores your habits, daily check-ins, streaks, focus sessions and reminder settings only on your device (local storage). This data is never sent to us or to any server operated by us. We have no accounts, no sign-up and no backend database.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App shows ads provided by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use your device's advertising ID and ad-related data (such as ad impressions and interactions) to provide and measure ads, as described in Google's Privacy Policy. You can reset or limit ad personalization in your device settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads).

3. Notifications

If you turn on the Daily Reminder, the App schedules a local notification on your device at the time you choose. No notification content leaves your device.

4. Data deletion

Because all App data is stored locally, you can delete it at any time by clearing the App's storage in your device settings or by uninstalling the App.

5. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

6. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The current version will always be available at this page, with the effective date shown above.
