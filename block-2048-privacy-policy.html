Privacy Policy — Block 2048

Effective date: November 10, 2026

Block 2048 ("the App") is a sliding-tile puzzle game. This policy explains what information the App handles and how.

1. No personal information is collected

The App does not collect, transmit or share any personal information. There is no account, no sign-up, and no server operated by us. The App works fully offline.

2. Information stored only on your device

The App stores your game data on the device itself (local storage): your current game in progress, score and best scores for each board size, game statistics (games played, wins, highest tile, total merges), your theme choice, unlocked badges, daily challenge progress, and your sound setting. This data never leaves the device. You can delete it at any time by clearing the App's storage in device settings or uninstalling the App.

3. No device access

The App does not access your camera, microphone, contacts, location, photos or files, and it does not request any device permissions. Game sounds are generated on the device and no audio is recorded.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

If ads are shown in the App, they are provided by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use your device's advertising ID and ad-related data (such as ad impressions and interactions) to provide and measure ads, as described in Google's Privacy Policy. You can reset or limit ad personalization in the device settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads).

5. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The current version will always be available at this page, with the effective date shown above.
